Welcome! I’m Sargol Mazraedoost, a researcher working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, chemistry, and biology.

I’m using this space to collect research notes, explain concepts I work with, and share what I learn along the way. Writing things down helps me see what I understand and which questions need more attention.

## What you’ll find here

- **Molecular AI:** how representations such as SMILES help models work with chemical structures.
- **Computational chemistry:** notes on molecular interactions, simulation, and prediction.
- **Research in practice:** methods, useful tools, and lessons from working with data.

I’ll aim for short explanations with examples and diagrams. Some notes will grow as I revisit a topic or learn a better way to explain it.

You can start with [my visual introduction to SMILES](/blog/smiles-a-visual-guide/), browse my [research projects](/projects/), or explore my [publications](/publications/).

Thanks for stopping by. If a post raises a question or connects with your work, [send me an email](mailto:sargol.mazraedoost7@gmail.com).
